# P23 hitset16 final record

## Scope and conclusion

This run tested minimum-hitting-set search over several induced universes in
the omega_16 type-M geometry. Every bank and every outer result is scoped to
the named universe; banks from different universes were never mixed.

No new sub-509 witness was found. No infeasibility result was proved for
508, 450, or 400 vertices. No claim is made for the full plane problem from
the reduced-universe experiments.

The decisive negative result is structural: even after reducing the
27,265-vertex radius-3 universe to a certified 5,751-vertex core, the
fractional hitting-set ceiling is far below 509. The method is therefore
not competitive on these bulk-pool universes.

## Soundness invariant

For every banked hyperedge `D`, the code holds a complete proper four-colouring
of the entire universe with `U \ D` conflict-free. Before every clause is
added, it asserts:

1. the colouring covers every vertex of `U`;
2. every edge with both endpoints outside `D` has differently coloured
   endpoints; and
3. `D` is disjoint from the current outer selection when generated by CEGAR.

Random sampled bank complements are independently sent to Kissat and must be
SAT. This invariant is the basis for every clause and every banked-bound
statement.

The outer model also uses the WLOG minimum-induced-degree-4 condition:
in a minimum vertex-critical non-4-colourable graph, a selected vertex cannot
have at most three selected neighbours because it could be coloured last.

## The LP ceiling

Let `d = min |D|` over a bank, and let `S` be the bank support. Setting

```text
x_v = 1/d  for every v in S
x_v = 0    otherwise
```

is a feasible fractional hitting set, because every banked hyperedge has at
least `d` support vertices. Therefore:

```text
LP optimum <= |S| / d.
```

Using the entire universe instead of the support gives the looser ceiling
`|U|/d`. For the 5,751 core and `d=107`, that ceiling is only 53.75 even
before support restriction; the actual final-bank support was 3,336, giving
the tighter ceiling 31.18. The measured LP optimum was only 3.75.

For the 2,167-witness optimizer bank, support was 2,110 and `d=135`, so the
support ceiling was 15.63. The later CEGAR clauses dropped `d` to 24 but
did not change the basic conclusion: the bank-driven LP remained tiny
relative to 509, and the curve flattened far below the target.

To make an LP-based certificate near 509 possible, a universe of size `n`
would require hyperedges averaging roughly at most `n/509`. A bulk pool
contains many vertex-disjoint non-4-colourable subgraphs, forcing every
whole-universe deletion set to be large. This is the hitting-set analogue
of the prior deletion calibration and explains why minimal-graph search must
start from nearly-critical universes rather than bulk disks.

An `OPTIMAL` result is exact only for the current partial bank and
support-restricted model. A time-limited `FEASIBLE` result supplies the
reported CP-SAT lower bound but does not prove optimality. Adding a sound
CEGAR hyperedge changes the outer model, so bounds are recorded per bank
state.

The explicit feasibility queries at 508, 450, and 400 all returned
`FEASIBLE` with colourable selections of sizes 19, 21, and 17,
respectively. No outer UNSAT result was obtained for 508, 450, 400, or any
comparable target. In particular, the run did not exhaust hyperedge
generation for those target selections, so no complete negative statement is
justified.

## Witness status

The outer selections encountered were four-colourable; none was a witness.
No new candidate entered the full gate.

The known 2,167-vertex witness was emitted into
`witness2167_exact.pkl` over `Q(sqrt(3),sqrt(7),sqrt(11))`, and exact
recomputation matched its 11,884 claimed edges. It is a known baseline, not
a new result. A fresh full Kissat/DRAT verification attempt for that known
graph was too expensive for this final run; consequently it is not claimed
as a newly verified output here.

No deletion or greedy minimization was used as a search method.

## What would be needed next

The next method should first construct a genuinely nearly-critical universe
with a small number of vertex-disjoint obstructions, not merely a smaller
induced core. Then the maximum-colourable-subset optimizer could produce
`|D|` near `n/509`, and a diverse bank could have an LP ceiling in the
hundreds. Without that structural change, increasing the number of clauses
cannot overcome the `|support|/min|D|` ceiling.
