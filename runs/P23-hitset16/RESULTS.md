## Orbit-closed witness variant

Closing the 2,167-vertex `greedy_t16a.pkl` witness under `lattice.orbit24`
produced `orbit_witness.pkl` with 22,135 vertices and 202,650 induced edges.
It is a strict superset of the known UNSAT witness, so it is UNSAT by
monotonicity. Direct Kissat status/proof attempts are recorded in
`logs/orbit_witness_sat_attempt.log` and
`logs/orbit_witness_proof_attempt.log`; both were stopped after the radius-3
proof had completed because the orbit-closed instance was substantially more
expensive. The orbit result does not rely on those incomplete attempts.

## Recommendation

Use the radius-3 pool (`wt_r3.pkl`) as the primary hitting-set universe:
it is the smallest tested radius that is independently SAT/DRAT certified
UNSAT, while retaining 13,633 orbit-closed base vertices. Keep
`orbit_witness.pkl` as a secondary structural-slack universe if the primary
pool is too restrictive.
